About the Club

Football for every age and ability in Linslade and the surrounding area

Who We Are

Linslade Galaxy FC is a community football club based at Cedars Upper School in Linslade, Bedfordshire. We field teams across a wide range of age groups — from U7 all the way through to U18 — across our mixed and girls sections.

Our teams compete across the Milton Keynes & District Development League (MKDDL), Chiltern Youth Football League, BMSL TT Couriers and Bedfordshire FA Girls Football League. Across all sections, we are proud to be an England Football Accredited Two Star club.

We believe football should be accessible, enjoyable, and developmental at every level. Whether your child is just starting out or looking to compete at a higher standard, there is a place for them at Linslade Galaxy.
